## Recording checks

- Use fictional `.test` recipients only; do not show Render or Google secrets.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%, notifications muted, and unrelated tabs closed.
- Record several takes. Reject any take at or above 3:00.
- Export 1080p with audible narration, upload to YouTube, and verify playback in
  incognito with captions optional but recommended.
